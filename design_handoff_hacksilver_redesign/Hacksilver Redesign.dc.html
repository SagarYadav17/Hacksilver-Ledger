Hacksilver Ledger — redesign concept

Full-app concept from the feature map, grounded in the Flutter repo: Outfit type and the app's teal M3 seed (ColorScheme.fromSeed) are kept, INR default per CurrencyProvider. Current features plus wanted-next (charts, transaction sheet, fast add, EMI timeline, sync modes, app lock). Biggest change: the NavigationDrawer becomes a 5-item bottom nav (Home · Activity · Loans · Accounts · More — Categories, Recurring, Sync, Settings under More). Offline-first — sync framed as optional. Scroll right for all screens; dark variants at the end. Tweaks: toggle notes / dark row.
